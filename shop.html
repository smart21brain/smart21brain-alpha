Everything a shop needs

One system, every part of running a shop

Built as a real, connected management system — not a basic prototype.

Point of Sale

A fast till — tap products or scan a barcode, take cash, mobile money, bank or card, and print a receipt in seconds.

Customers

Save every customer automatically. See what they've bought, what they owe, and receive part-payments toward their balance.

Products & Stock

Track stock automatically with every sale. Get warned before something runs out, and see the exact value of what's on your shelves.

Credit & Debts

Sell on credit with a credit limit per customer, and a clear list of everyone who owes you — nothing gets forgotten.

Expenses

Record rent, transport, salaries and every other cost, so your profit numbers reflect what the shop really makes.

Reports & Dashboard

A live dashboard with charts for sales and payments, plus a full reports centre — profit & loss, stock, debtors — exportable to PDF or Excel.

Roles & Permissions

Owners, managers and cashiers each see only what they need — you decide exactly what each role can do, including who sees cost and profit.

Activity Log

Every sale, cancellation, stock change and staff action is logged — a plain record of who did what, and when.

How it works

From sign-up to your first sale

Every step is connected — a sale taken at the till shows up immediately in stock, the customer's profile and the dashboard.

1

Register your shop in under a minute

2

Add your products (or start from ready-made categories)

3

Save your customers as you serve them

4

Sell at the till — stock and receipts update automatically

5

Record expenses as they come up

6

Check your real profit any time from the reports centre

Built for any shop, not just one

Every shop that registers gets its own private workspace — its own customers, products, staff and settings, completely separate from every other shop. Owners can change the branding, currency, VAT rate, payment methods and what each role is allowed to do.
