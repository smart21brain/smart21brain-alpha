Smart21brain Stationery

Stationery

Your Digital Stationery Assistant — one platform for sales, printing, passport photos, design, documents and an AI-powered helper for the stationery shop floor.

Smart21brain Stationery brings the POS counter, the photocopier, the photo studio and the design table into a single workflow, so a shop owner or operator can run the whole business from one screen.

Launch Stationery OS Learn more

Free to try — sign in with your smart21brain account and a starter shop is set up for you automatically.

More than a passport-photo tool

Smart21brain Stationery is designed as an all-in-one digital stationery operating system — it connects business management, printing and photo services, and a training academy under one roof, so an operator rarely needs to leave the app to get a job done.

The engine at the centre of it all is simple: every job — a print run, a passport photo, a design, a photocopy — becomes an order that flows from customer to service to payment to receipt, keeping sales, stock and reports in sync automatically.

How the pieces connect

  1. Business Management + Tools Center + Academy all feed into one dashboard
  2. The AI Assistant sits across all three — troubleshooting machines, drafting summaries, giving business advice
  3. A Universal Order Engine (Customer → Service → Order → Payment → Receipt) ties every module together
Core Modules

Everything a stationery shop needs, in one place

Business

Sales & POS

Dashboard, mauzo & manunuzi, cashbook, debts/credit, receipts and invoices — every sale computed automatically from service × quantity.

Business

Inventory & Stock

Track A4/A3 paper, ink, toner, laminating pouches and more, with automatic low-stock alerts and supplier & purchase records.

Print Center

Printing & Document Services

Printing, photocopy, scanning, lamination, binding and booklet creation — plus business cards, flyers, posters, invitations and stickers.

Photo Studio

Passport & ID Photo Studio

Upload → background removal → configurable size presets for passport, visa, ID and student photos → auto A4 layout (4/6/8/12 per page) → print-ready export.

Design Studio

Templates & Design Tools

A mini design canvas with text, shapes, QR codes and templates for business cards, certificates, invitations and letterheads.

Document Center

PDF & Document Tools

Merge, split, compress, convert (PDF ↔ Word/JPG), watermark, protect and OCR — turning scans into searchable, editable documents.

Online Services

Application Assistant

A guided checklist for TRA, BRELA, NIDA, passport, visa, business license and school/job applications — forms, documents and tracking notes, without touching restricted government systems directly.

Smart21brain AI

AI Stationery Assistant

Guides operators through machine troubleshooting, drafts business summaries, suggests pricing and stock decisions, and walks customers through the passport-photo workflow.

Smart21brain Academy

Machine Training & Courses

Short courses on Word, Excel, graphic design and printing technology, plus a machine encyclopedia covering printers, photocopiers, laminators and binders — parts, setup, maintenance and troubleshooting.

Analytics

Business Analytics

Today's sales, expenses and profit at a glance, plus sales/profit graphs, best-selling services and employee performance.

People

Customers & Employees

Customer profiles with order and payment history, plus role-based access for Owner, Manager, Operator, Designer, Accountant and Technician.

Shop

Online Orders & Shop

Customers upload a document, set quantity, see the price and track their order — Received → Processing → Ready → Collected. Later: a stationery marketplace for paper, ink and accessories.
