Our story

Every child deserves a brilliant teacher

Smart21Brain started with a simple question: what if quality education felt as fun and accessible as a favourite game? Here's what we've built since.

Mission

Make brilliant teaching reachable for every learner

We partner with real teachers to turn classroom-tested lessons into videos, books, games and quizzes that work on any device — from a village primary school to a family living room.

What we stand for

Our values

Safety first

Every piece of content is reviewed before it reaches a child's screen, and parents stay in control of what's watched and played.

Joyful learning

Games, streaks and badges aren't decoration — they're how we keep curiosity alive lesson after lesson.

Built with educators

Every course is written or reviewed by a qualified teacher — technology supports the classroom, it doesn't replace it.
